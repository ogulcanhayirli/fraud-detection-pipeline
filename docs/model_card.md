## Overview

- **Task:** card transaction fraud detection (binary classification)
- **Dataset:** Sparkov synthetic transactions (`kartik2112/fraud-detection`)
- **Intended use:** portfolio demonstration. Not fit for production use on real
  payment data; the training data is synthetic and its fraud patterns are
  generator artefacts, not observed criminal behaviour.

## Features

Defined in `src/features/` and imported unchanged by both training and serving;
the exact contract is `src.features.FEATURE_COLUMNS` (19 features). Every
history-dependent feature is computed over a card's strictly earlier
transactions only. `gender` is available in the raw data and deliberately not
used; `merchant`, `job`, `city` and `state` await a causal target encoder.
